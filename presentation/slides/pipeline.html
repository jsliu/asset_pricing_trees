Pipeline

From characteristics to an SDF and stock scores

1

Grow trees

36 sets: size plus 2 of 9 characteristics, depth 4

2

Residualise

r − B·F, with B from the training window; alpha kept

3

Prune each tree

Elastic-net SDF, grid search, run in parallel

4

Combine

Pool the selected nodes and prune again

5

Score

Node weights β give monthly returns and stock scores

Refit every January on all months before the test month; the months in between reuse that fit.

Outputs: Return (factor-hedged), Return_mkt_adj (market-adjusted) and stock scores for every stock in the universe.

Each January the tree returns are residualised on the nine factor returns with betas from the training window, each tree is pruned on its own, and the survivors are pooled and pruned once more. The final node weights give the SDF return and the stock scores for the following twelve months.
