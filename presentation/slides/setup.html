Data and model

Data

  • Monthly US characteristic files, 1963–2016, full universe (no large-cap filter)
  • 9 characteristics: accruals, book-to-market, idiosyncratic volatility, momentum, profitability, investment, short- and long-term reversal, turnover; plus size
  • About 6,800 stocks a month with returns, about 2,950 with every characteristic
  • Zero market-cap rows dropped; returns net of the equal-weighted market

Model

  • 36 tree sets: size plus each pair of characteristics, depth 4, median splits, nodes of at least 50 stocks, equal-weighted
  • Elastic-net SDF over a mean-shrinkage × ridge grid with time-series cross-validation; 5–50 portfolios kept
  • Refit every January on all months before the test month
  • Out of sample from January 1980 to December 2016: 444 months
