Stock scores

Three ways to turn node weights into stock scores

ScoreHow each stock is scoredRole
norm_scoreNode weight × geometric mean of the stock's market-wide characteristic ranksOriginal score
sdf_weightNode weight β spread equally over the node's stocks, summed over nodesThe SDF portfolio exactly
size_oriented_scoreThe same β per node, tilted towards stocks deepest inside the node (ranks within the parent node, in the split's direction)Default score
size_oriented_normsize_oriented_score rank-normalised to ±3.5; stocks in no node sit at 0For combining with factor scores

The original score used raw ranks whatever side of a split a node was on, so it could favour the wrong stocks. The new scores keep each node's total weight equal to its SDF weight.

sdf_weight is exactly the SDF: holding stocks in these weights reproduces the market-adjusted SDF return to within rounding. size_oriented_score redistributes each node's weight towards the stocks that express the node's characteristics most strongly, measured inside the parent node the tree split.
